# Community code of conduct

We welcome developers and users of all backgrounds. Be respectful, constructive and patient. Critique ideas and code rather than people, respect differing experiences, and give credit where due.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disruption and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through a private contact method listed on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/mehmood22), if available. Do not publish sensitive details in issues. If no private contact is available, use GitHub's reporting tools for platform abuse.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, warn participants or restrict participation depending on severity. Reports should be handled discreetly, fairly and without retaliation. This policy applies to project issues, discussions, pull requests and other official community spaces.
